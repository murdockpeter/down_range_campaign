The modular building and EW operator STL files were converted to coordinate-normalized OBJ derivatives for Unity. No commercial use is intended.

The LPM Field Camo Texture and USMC Field Camo Texture are project-created derivative paint swatches informed by the palettes of the licensed Down Range miniature textures. They are applied through an object-space triplanar shader to models whose STL/3MF sources contain no authored UV or material data.
