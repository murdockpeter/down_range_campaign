# Mission 01: Silent Lantern — TTS Playthrough

## Board

Use `assets/maps/silent-lantern-tts-map-v1.png` as a 72 × 48 inch custom board. The texture is 1536 × 1024 (3:2). North is the top edge.

The image is terrain-only. Add physical or invisible TTS objects over buildings, fences, ridge crests, and dense tree groups wherever three-dimensional line-of-sight blocking is desired.

## Terrain key

- **Southwest farm:** Civilian-sensitive terrain and BLUE infiltration/exfiltration area.
- **Western woods:** Concealment. Dense stands block line of sight; movement through them is impaired.
- **Nūmerne ridge:** Elevated observation ground. Use the pale rocky crest as the elevation footprint.
- **Central fields:** Open danger area. Drainage ditches offer concealment to prone troops and impair movement when crossed.
- **Relay compound:** Fenced objective area. The southern break/gate is the normal entrance.
- **Eastern paved road:** Fast RED reinforcement route and exposed avenue of approach.

## BLUE force

- Scout leader with radio
- Three riflemen, including one automatic rifleman
- UAS operator with one quadcopter
- One additional radio operator or medic

Deploy within 8 inches of the southwest or west edge, no closer than 24 inches to the relay fence. BLUE may split its entry between the farm and western woods.

## RED force — facilitator information

- Two-person security team inside the relay fence
- Two-person patrol beginning on the northern track
- One operator or technician in the equipment hut
- Optional two-person QRF entering from the eastern road after the alarm

RED begins hidden unless visible to BLUE. The facilitator records concealed positions and patrol movement.

## Objectives

1. Establish an observation post touching the Nūmerne ridge crest.
2. Maintain observation of the relay compound for two uninterrupted friendly turns.
3. Identify the antenna, security element, and southern access route.
4. Exit at least 75% of BLUE from the southwest or west edge by the end of Turn 8.
5. Avoid a confirmed alarm.

## Alarm triggers

The alarm becomes confirmed when any one of these occurs:

- RED successfully signals after seeing BLUE.
- Unsuppressed gunfire or an explosion occurs.
- BLUE crosses the relay fence.
- The facilitator rules that repeated exposed UAS activity has been detected.

When confirmed, release the QRF from the eastern road on the following RED turn. RED patrols may move directly toward the last known BLUE position.

## Suggested turn flow

| Turns | Phase | Expected decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Infiltration | Farm drainage route or slower wooded approach |
| 3–5 | Observation | Hold the ridge, reposition the UAS, or risk moving closer |
| 6–8 | Exfiltration | Leave early with partial intelligence or remain to complete collection |

## Facilitator notes

The scenario is not an assault on the compound. A BLUE withdrawal with good intelligence and no alarm is the best result. A firefight that destroys RED but exposes the reconnaissance effort should reduce campaign momentum even if BLUE wins tactically.

For a conventional two-player version, place all RED models openly but allow the RED player to write down one false patrol marker and the QRF entry turn before play.
